Solar Water Pump

Diesel pumps cost money every hour they run and grid supply to farms is rarely available when the crop needs water. A solar water pump irrigates on sunlight, with zero fuel cost and no dependence on the feeder schedule.

Green Solar Energy installs surface and submersible solar pumps from 1HP to 10HP with VFD controllers, dry-run protection and remote monitoring — and assists with PM-KUSUM scheme applications where applicable.

What You Get With This Service

1HP to 10HP

AC and DC, surface and submersible pump sets for borewells, open wells and canals.

VFD Controller

Variable frequency drive with dry-run and overload protection built in.

KUSUM Assistance

Documentation support for PM-KUSUM scheme benefits where eligible.

Tracking Structures

Optional manual tracking structures to extend daily pumping hours.

Key Benefits

  • Zero diesel cost — irrigation runs entirely on sunlight
  • Water available exactly when the crop needs it
  • No dependence on farm feeder supply timings
  • Low maintenance with no engine servicing
  • Life of 20+ years for panels, 5+ years for the pump set
  • Service support at your field, not just on the phone
