Answers to the Questions We Hear Most

Everything a first-time solar buyer in Madhya Pradesh asks us

For a typical household in Jaora with a monthly bill of ₹3,000–₹5,000, a 3kW rooftop system usually brings the bill down to the minimum fixed charges — a saving of 80% to 100%. With the PM Surya Ghar subsidy of up to ₹78,000, most homes recover their entire investment within 3 to 4 years and then generate free power for the remaining 20+ years of system life.
The subsidy is credited directly to your bank account after your system is installed and the net meter is commissioned. You need a residential electricity connection, a shadow-free roof, Aadhaar and a bank account. Green Solar Energy is a registered vendor and files the entire application — portal registration, DISCOM feasibility, inspection and disbursal follow-up — on your behalf at no extra charge.
An on-grid system works with the DISCOM supply and gives the maximum bill savings, but shuts down during a power cut. An off-grid system runs entirely on battery and is used where there is no reliable grid. A hybrid system does both — it saves on your bill during the day and keeps your home running on battery during outages. We recommend the right one after studying your load and outage pattern.
Panels need cleaning every 15–30 days and an annual electrical health check — that is all. Modules carry a 25-year linear performance warranty, inverters typically 5–10 years, and Green Solar Energy adds a written 5-year system and workmanship warranty. We also offer AMC packages that include scheduled cleaning, performance audits and priority breakdown support.
